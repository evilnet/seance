# ps theme: implementation map

Where the `ps` theme's pieces live, moved out of `CLAUDE.md` on 2026-11-02. Specs: `docs/projects/ps-theme.md`, `docs/projects/ps-ng.md`.

## The `ps` theme

**The `ps` theme** (`client/themes/ps.css`, `docs/projects/ps-theme.md`; forked from the `<3` theme, whose design record is `docs/projects/heart-theme.md`; plan 1, "the seam", landed 2026-09-24, plan 2, "the chrome", 2026-09-25, plan 3, "the plains", and plan 4, "the rest", 2026-09-26) — peace on the plains: a live scene behind the whole app (sky, sun, moon, stars, clouds, the land and river, a yurt, the birds, the day's weather), the same for every channel, on the viewer's own local clock, replacing the old per-conversation meadow. **The hook** (`client/js/themeScene.ts`) is the one place any theme can bring page elements of its own: a table (`SCENES`) of theme names to a lazily-imported scene module — only `ps` is in it — mounted into `#theme-scene`, an empty `<div>` in `client/index.html` that `style.css` keeps `display: none` for every theme — only `ps.css` shows it, so the element stays invisible in the instant between a theme switch and its scene's removal; `settings.ts` calls `themeScene.setTheme()` when a theme is applied, `boot.ts` wires `installThemeSceneHooks()` to `visibilitychange` and to the window's attention (focus, blur, input) (a bfcache `pageshow` catches up too, and a chunk that failed to load is retried on `online` or when the page is shown), and `App.vue` reports what kind of conversation is open. **The modules** under `client/js/scenes/ps/` are Vue-free and DOM-free but `watchers.ts`, so mocha loads them directly: `engine.ts` (a `Date` in; sunrise/sunset/season/weather and the sun's and moon's positions out), `colour.ts` (sRGB and OKLab mixing, contrast), `palette.ts` (the colour table, the day's levels — the river's water, the flowers, the snow on the roof, the fireflies, the wind and sway, the heat, the veil — and the derived land and yurt colours), `plains.ts` (the markup of the land and river, the near grass, the fireflies, the yurt and its smoke, five clouds, the wet weather's own — `weatherClouds`: four more, bigger and lower, in rain and a storm, and behind them a still overcast deck — a storm's, and plain rain's lighter one (a 22 % band, flatter billows; the user: "yes to the rain deck") — whose billows' height is tied to their width (in `cqw`, centred on the band's edge), rebuilt with the weather layer in `.ps-overcast`, one `.ps-overcast-set` per day's weather, replaced at once — and `weatherLayers`), `birds.ts` (the user's N2 moonlit skeins by night and around sunset, a buzzard and skylarks by day, none in rain or a storm, and `birdsAt`, their timing), `grounds.ts` (the one ground list the page and the tools share, and the words' treatment), `glass.ts` (the luminous day glass's tints), `layers.ts` (which animated layers are in the render tree), `markup.ts` (the scene's markup and the moment's custom properties), `publish.ts` (what the scene publishes on `<html>`) and `watchers.ts` (the land's stretch, the composer's height, the overcast). **The scene** (`client/js/scenes/psng/scene.ts`, "NG": spec `docs/projects/ps-ng.md`, plans `ps-ng-plan-1.md` to `ps-ng-plan-4.md`; it replaced the classic renderer and its stepper on 2026-10-17) is built to cost what coffee costs. It builds twelve layers once from `markup.ts` (the Milky Way, stars, glow, moon, sun, clouds, the ground group — land and river, fireflies, yurt, smoke, the animal layer — the near grass, the skeins, the day birds, the veil, the weather layer; geometry in `%`, travel in `cqw`/`cqh` since `#theme-scene` is a size container, small marks in `rem`) with every SMIL element removed and every svg's clock paused, adopts its own stylesheet (`psng/styles.ts` `NG_STILL_CSS`: every animation and transition off — the yurt's fade excepted — and the in-flight layers out), and ticks once a minute (and again the instant the page becomes visible) to write the engine's and palette's output as custom properties on its own root. The weather layer holds the day's weather alone and is rebuilt when the day's weather changes (local midnight with the page open, or waking into another day), for the layout of that moment, its rain in viewport-sized tiles (`psng/rain.ts`, periods 0.52/0.62/0.71 s, the drops falling straight with the slant drawn in) and never a heat haze (`ps-hot` is never set); a phone gets half the particles and half the fireflies. The yurt stands with its centre 22 % of the window's width in from the message column's far end — the scene's edge, or the user list's inner edge while a channel shows it (`html:has(.userlist-open …)`, `--ps-yurt-in`) — clamped whole on screen, in CSS alone (`ps.css` `--ps-yurt-x`; 2026-10-17, the user: right-anchor it and lose the JS), so a sidebar opening or closing never moves it; it never slides, and a move (the user list toggled, a resize) is a cut. A layer outside its window (the stars, fireflies and smoke by day, the sun at night, the skeins by day, the larks out of season, the seeds while the wind shows none, the heat band off a hot midday) leaves the render tree at once (`.ps-off`), and the scene states its own `box-sizing` (the app's `box-sizing: inherit` made every restyle about 4× dearer). **Motion comes back group by group as `ps.css`'s own CSS animations** (spec §9): nine groups (`psng/groups.ts`: clouds, stars, fireflies, smoke, grass, weather, skeins, daybirds, sun), each running while its class `psng-m-<group>` is on `#theme-scene`, every delay moved by `--psng-t` (the seconds since local midnight of the day the scene mounted, less the time spent frozen, written once on each move out of still and never per frame or on the minute tick, so a loop resumes where the clock puts it). Only `transform` and `opacity` animate and no moving box is larger than the viewport: every wingbeat is a stack of pose copies swapped by `opacity` (`styles.ts` `poseKeyframes`: 80 beats to one iteration, since WebKit does main-thread work at every iteration of every animation, and shown 0.999 / hidden 0.001, since an opacity of exactly 1 or 0 at a swap repaints the bird; spec §10.5), a lark's three wing sets three HTML state boxes (`poses.ts` `larkStates`) on linear keyframes, never `steps()` and never an animation inside an `<svg>`, the buzzard's path a CSS loop on a fitted circle, the near grass sways as one wrapper (`div.psng-sway`, flowers included), and the sun's fire is `psng/flame.ts`, a canvas painted at 24 frames a second from a sprite of the loop's frames (`fireDriver`), running only in full with the sun's group and gate on. The frost snaps, the day's clouds swap without a fade and the cranes never glide and the skein birds no longer wander. **The theme's own settings** (Settings → Appearance under the theme select, shown while ps is the theme; spec §10.4): `psWords` ("Words over the plains": `bubbles`/`glass`/`scrim`/`outline`/`rim`/`glow`, default `bubbles`, `helpers/psWords.ts` `normalizePsWords`, above), `psAnimation` ("Scene animation": `auto`/`full`/`calm`/`still`, default `auto` → the host's `motion`, `themeScene.ts` `SceneMotion`; a stored older value is read, never rewritten, through `normalizeSceneMotion`: `60`/`24` as Auto, `1s`/`sparse` as Calm, `off` as Still), `psPauseWhenAway` (off: the host always says attended) and `psGroupMessages` (off: no `previous-source`); browser check `tools/scenarios/ps-scene-settings.mjs` (the words setting too: each of the six chosen through its radio and read off the page); `tools/scenarios/ps-words-cost.mjs` measures the outline, bubbles and the glass column with the scene in motion. The tier (`psng/tiers.ts` `tierFor`): Auto is Full on a computer or tablet, Calm on a touch phone, one step less on low power (`helpers/power.ts`); **still** for a hidden or unattended page, a query, reduced motion, the setting at Still or the phone's open drawer — no animation object at all (never `pause()`: a paused animation still costs 19 % of a core on an iPhone); a rest that ends on its own (hidden, unattended, a query) **freezes**: each running animation's `transform`/`opacity` is pinned inline, `psng-frozen` on the root makes it `animation: none` (and hides the lightning, which could hold lit), the group classes stay and the frozen time comes off `--psng-t`, so the loops go on from where they stood, while reduced motion, Still and the drawer drop the group classes and snap; **calm** (two of the sky's five clouds alone, none of the wet weather's, nothing falling on a wet day — plan 5, spec §11; `psng-calm` on the root); **full** otherwise. `localStorage["thelounge.state.psngGroups"]` (a comma list, read at mount) narrows the groups for measuring; set but empty, nothing moves. A visible page nobody attends to rests (`themeScene.ts` `createAttention`: 15 s after the window loses the focus, `UNFOCUSED_REST_MS`, or 2 minutes without pointer, key, wheel or touch input, `IDLE_REST_MS`; any input, the focus or the page coming back on its tab runs it again from where it stood) — a desktop window behind others or on a second screen fires no `visibilitychange`, and without this it animated, and re-blurred the glass over it, all day. `browser-drive.mjs` keeps automation attended (a synthetic `pointermove` every 10 s — a headless page has no focus, and focus emulation would change `document.hasFocus()` for every other scenario) unless the scenario exports `sceneRest = true`; browser check `tools/scenarios/scene-rest.mjs` (no ircd); under reduced motion what exists only in flight (the birds, the rain, snow and seeds, the lightning, the smoke) leaves the render tree, while the veil, the still fireflies and the hour's colours stay; in a query it stands behind frosted glass, completely still (`ps-private` on the root: its one wrapper `.ps-frost` blurred 1.125rem, desaturated to 0.85 and scaled 1.2, frozen as a hidden page's is while the minute's tick keeps its colours on the hour; spec §5.7). It publishes on `<html>` `data-ps-light` (`day`/`night`), `data-ps-text` (`ink`/`light`), `--ps-halo` (the ink treatment's glow), `--canvas-bg-color` (the hour's sky-top colour, also read by the iOS status bar tint), by day the four glass tints, and `ps-form-tall` (below); it also writes that sky-top into `<meta name="theme-color">` and hands the value it found back on destroy, unless something else wrote the meta since (`configuration.ts` gives `ps` `#3f8fe6`, the fallback's canvas, until the scene loads). **Without the chunk** (still loading, failed, or an older build), `ps.css` alone paints a fixed, legible daylight sky and ink text — never an unstyled or midnight page. **The words over the plains are a setting** since plan 6 (2026-10-21, `docs/projects/ps-words.md`): `psWords` → `html[data-ps-words]`, six values in two families, a missing attribute read as the default. The **surface family** — `bubbles` (the default: every `.msg` row on a translucent card, `width: fit-content`, paper `rgb(255 251 244 / var(--ps-g-tint-card, var(--ps-s-tint)))` with ink by day, `rgb(12 17 32 / var(--ps-s-tint))` with light words at night, the load-more button and the marker labels as pills, the message toolbar anchored to the row's start), `glass` (the message list `#chat .chat-content > .chat` on the chrome's glass at the float tint, the card's flat tint and no blur on the phone) and `scrim` (a three-stop gradient from the near edge, 0.3 of the card tint at the far edge, mirrored under `:root[dir="rtl"]`) — draws nothing on the words and keys the column's own surfaces (`--window-bg-color`, `--composer-bg`, the washes, the links' underline, high contrast) on `data-ps-light`; its colours are the generated `ps:surface-palette` block (below), and `--ps-g-tint-card` is a fifth surface of `glass.ts`, solved flat per step over every ground, under the paper and under a mention's wash, for the block's lightest text (`DAY_CARD_TEXT` at 4.6) and faint ink (`DAY_CARD_FAINT` at 3), at or under the block's declared 0.66 (`--ps-s-tint`; 0.74 at night). The **shadow family** keys on the ink window as before: `outline` (what shipped until plan 6: white all day and all night with a close dark shadow over the user's eight-way 78 % rim and a faint 28 % second ring, and dark ink `#1b2638` on a pale halo only inside the day's ink window, `grounds.ts` `inkWindow`, in practice snowy days, changing at most once each side of noon — the user's "white sooner", then "A"), `rim` (one 1 px ring at 60 % in `--ps-rim`, the hour's sky-top at 0.45 of its OKLCH lightness, published by the scene with `rimFor`, fallback `#123a6e`; the ink unchanged) and `glow` (four blurred black layers, no ring; a four-layer aura of the halo under the ink). The rim and the glow keep the outline's message palette: their strengths are measured (`tools/ps/calibrate.mjs`, α 0.4164 and 0.1372, the aura 0.1503) and `test/scenes/ps/legibility.ts` pins each kind's worst figure over every light ground and the count of grounds that leave white under 4.5 (white 2.35 : 1 under the rim and 1.37 under the glow over a white cloud), by the user's choice of a softer look; the scrim's far edge is pinned the same way (2.12 by day, 1.42 at night). **The chrome is glass over the plains**: the sidebar, the channel header, the user list, `#form` (one surface with its bars and the typing strip) and the reaction chips are a tint (`rgb(255 251 244 / var(--ps-g-tint))` by day, `rgb(12 17 32 / var(--ps-g-tint-a))` under `data-ps-light="night"`) under `backdrop-filter: blur(0.625rem) var(--ps-g-lift,) saturate(1.12)` — never on `#status-bar-tint`, `html` or `body`, which iOS would stop sampling. By day the glass is luminous (the user's pick): `--ps-g-lift` is `brightness(1.3)` on `:root[data-ps-light="day"]` alone, and each surface reads the tint the scene solves for it each 5-minute step (`--ps-g-tint-header`, `-composer`, `-side`, `-float`: the lowest within 0.40–0.78 that holds the soft ink at 4.6 and the join green's marks at 3.1 through the filter over what can stand behind it); without the scene every surface takes the generated `--ps-g-tint-a` (0.78) unbrightened, and the night glass is the generated 0.74, unbrightened. A composer risen above the near grass (`ps-form-tall`, set by the scene) and a user list laid over a narrow chat take the chips' float tint. On the phone layout the always-on glass (the header, the composer, the chips) has no backdrop filter and the generated tint (the measured budget's pre-decided fallback, spec §10), while the open drawer and the user list laid over the chat keep the blur and the brightening on the chips' tint (the float tint as shipped, the user's pick over 70 and 78; the drawer's blur holds until its slide has taken it off screen). What holds dense text (settings, Help, the changelog, the connect form, the context menu, the Mentions popover, the autocomplete, the emoji picker, the upload preview, the dialogs, the push prompt, the jump-to-recent disc) is `--ps-g-solid` (`#fbf8f2`/`#121827`), and so are a native select and its options, in the ink (Chromium draws the list from their fills; the field's night wash had left light text on a light list); the message toolbar is solid but follows the column's treatment. Two full palettes (every token coffee.css reads, on `:root` and `:root[data-ps-light="night"]`) flip over `--ps-flip` (0.8 s, 0 under reduced motion), and `prefers-reduced-transparency: reduce` makes the glass solid in every state (its block is `ps.css`'s last rule and names the phone's overlay states itself). Every wash on the glass moves the ground away from its text (lighter by day, toward black at night — the user's pick), the chrome writes and marks in a generated text accent (`--ps-g-accent-text`; the spec accent stays for the open row's marker, the caret and the tints), unread and mention share one badge, the phone's scrim starts at the open drawer's edge so the drawer frosts the plains and not the scrim, a network that is down fades `.chat-content > .chat` (and the jump-to-recent disc's arrow) rather than `.chat-content`, which would make it a backdrop root and take the user list's blur, the desktop's windows sit flush with the sidebar, square and flat (no `day.css` card), and an own message's echo settles up from its pending copy's 0.55 (`ps-settle`) rather than fading in from nothing. **Embers** replace the `<3` glitter (CSS only, pseudo-elements, `@keyframes ps-ember`): the echo of an own message (`.msg.self:not(.pending):last-child`) lights four sparks and an entering reaction chip two, in a channel's view only and never under reduced motion; the chip's rule restates `reaction-pop` beside a hold (`ps-ember-hold`) so Vue keeps the enter class, and the text column clips with `overflow-clip-margin: 3.25rem` meanwhile instead of lifting its anti-Zalgo clip. **Type** (the user's picks, 2026-09-26): Source Sans 3 500 (bold 700) for the words and Newsreader 700 (the static 16 pt instance, the bytes the user picked) for the chrome's names — channel and network names, the header title (a sender's nick over its message is the words' face, bold, since 2026-10-09) — as Google Fonts' woff2 files in `client/themes/ps/`, Latin, Latin Extended and Vietnamese as separate files — a face needs all three, so prove coverage by rendering; a family with a space or a figure is quoted; `node tools/ps/fetch-fonts.mjs` fetches them and rewrites the `@font-face` rules between the `ps:fonts` markers; every other script, Cyrillic and Greek included, falls back to the system stack. A nick inside a message's text (`.content .user`: a mention, an action's actor, a join or quit line's nick) is the words' face, coloured and bold (the user's I2). **Messages are stacked rows** (the user's B, 2026-10-06, and the time first, 2026-10-08; spec §8): every row starts with its time in a column as wide in every row as the clock setting's widest time (`--ps-time-w` on `#chat`, `minmax(var(--ps-time-w), max-content)`); a message is time, then nick, and the text on the next line under the nick; a run of one sender's lines (`previous-source`, `helpers/messageRuns.ts` `continuesRun`: the same nick, plain messages, at most `RUN_GAP_MS` = 10 minutes apart; `test/helpers/messageRuns.ts`) shows the nick once, each later line's time in its column shown on hover or long press; every other row (join, mode, action) is time, icon (the `.from::before` glyph), text, the icon sharing the text's cell so the text block starts under the nicks and only its first line steps past the icon; a condensed summary's text stands with the nicks. CSS only, alike at every pane width (`#chat .chat` selectors win over `style.css`'s narrow inline flow), so a nick is never cut (`test/themes/ps.ts` "stacked message rows"). **The generated colours** (`tools/ps/palette-blocks.ts`, formerly `message-palette.ts`; regenerate with `npx tsx tools/ps/palette-blocks.ts --write`, idempotent, about five minutes) are three blocks in `ps.css`: `ps:message-palette` (every colour the message column reads in both treatments, its nick sweeps, the code tokens and `--ps-code-bg`, the box the column's code sits on) `ps:glass-palette` (the tint's opacity, the soft ink, the badge fill, the text accent, the chrome's day and night nick sweeps) and `ps:surface-palette` (plan 6: every column token and the 32 nicks for the words plain on a card, the glass column or the scrim, by day at the declared 0.66 over every ground, under a mention's wash and on the glass column at the float tint through the lift, and at night at the lowest opacity from 0.62 at which the ink holds and the glass soft fits rule 2, 0.74; `--ps-s-tint`). Each keeps its base's OKLCH hue and chroma (the nicks from one 32-hue table; every base lives in the generator, which reads `ps.css` only to replace its blocks, so retinting the file alone never reaches them) and only its lightness is solved, to 4.6 — the 4.5:1 floor plus a 0.1 rounding margin — against the worst ground its surface meets in `tools/ps/legibility.ts`'s dense sweep (every day, every 5 minutes, all six weathers): every area of `grounds.ts`'s one ground list (the sky, the moon's disc, the sun's core above the horizon line, the land, the river, the yurt and its glows, the pool at its door, the clouds, the smoke; under the veil on a veiled day), through the column's text layer at the strength `tools/ps/calibrate.mjs` measured from rendered pixels on real chat phrases (`ALPHA_HALO` 0.1614, `ALPHA_SHADOW` 0.6 with the second ring — measured 0.6039 on the plan-4 faces, over the cap — each `min(0.6, …)`), or through the glass tint. Under the light treatment every coloured word comes out nearly white, so links there are underlined, and the 32 nicks are the user's names-large set (3:1 as WCAG large text) with the `sky` set (4.5:1 over the sky) below the default font-size step. A spec colour that misses its floor is fixed by raising the glass opacity to its cap (0.78 day, 0.74 night) or moving a secondary colour at most 0.08 in OKLCH lightness (rule 2); anything more is the user's call (docs/projects/ps-theme.md §11). `test/scenes/ps/legibility.ts` holds both blocks and the spec's own fixed colours to the plain floors over the sparse sweep (every 7th day plus the season anchors, every 10 minutes) and every moment a block header pins, the luminous glass at the tints the scene computes and the phone's unfiltered glass at its declared tint, and the rule-2 bound (the generated secondaries and the chrome's hand-moved ones); `test/themes/ps.ts` holds the chrome's floors on the solid, the field, the glass and each wash, over the same pinned moments, that no rule painting a glass surface outranks reduced transparency, and plan 4's rules (the embers, the reduced-motion layers, the select, the faces, the in-message nick's reach, the stacked message rows). **The animals are switched off, not removed**: the generated silhouette SVGs (`tools/heart/generate.mjs` from the rigs under `tools/heart/rigs/` — the directory keeps its historical name; files committed under `client/themes/ps/`, README in `tools/heart/`) and the scene's fixed cast (horse, bunny, a deer far off) are assigned to `--ps-slot-a/-b/-f` on `:root`; `#theme-scene .ps-animals`'s own rule only paints them, and one block right after it sets those same three custom properties to `none`, so none is painted or fetched; delete it and they come back. Tests: `test/scenes/ps/{engine,palette,plains,birds,grounds,glass,layers,scene,legibility}.ts`, `test/scenes/psng/*.ts` (`mount.ts` runs the scene's `mount` on the shared stand-in page, `test/scenes/standin.ts`: the still census, the gates, the tiers and the freeze, `--psng-t`, the fire, the drawer, destroy leaving nothing), `test/tests/themeScene.ts`, `test/themes/ps.ts`, `test/tools/heart/`; browser: `tools/scenarios/theme-ps.mjs` (the scene's mount and unmount, the published values by hour, a hidden page stopping it, the daylight fallback, that no animal file is fetched, the chrome — the fonts drawn, glass by day and at night, reduced transparency, `theme-color`, the phone's fallback and overlays — and, on a clock it pins with a `Date` shim, the plains: only the day's weather in the page, the words by weather and hour, the weather rebuilt across local midnight, a phone's halved counts, the yurt never caught between places, each cloud's loop off both edges and their rest under reduced motion, the drawer's blur through its slide, a query frosted and still, cold boots at 4× with `ps.css` held back (the yurt's place in the first laid-out frame), the pending message's settle, the wet weather's clouds and no bird, reduced motion's stand-down in every weather, the embers, a reply quote kept in its column, the select by the hour, the value `#status-bar-tint` paints, and the glyph samples — rendered words, times and nicks against their ring one CSS px out, `calibrate.mjs`'s twin method, at noon, golden hour, sunset, dusk, midnight and first light on a clear summer day and a snowy winter one, each at its floor; screenshots across a day and the seasons; the stacked rows: the nick, then the time, the text under them, a continuation line's header hidden, an icon before the time; and the six words treatments chosen in turn at noon and at midnight, with the glyph samples run under the rim and the glow as well — every inherited check runs under `outline`, which the scenario seeds) and `tools/scenarios/ps-ng.mjs` (also `--mobile --width=390 --height=844` and the landscape phone `--mobile --width=932 --height=430`; its header lists every check: the full census at noon, 23:00, a storm day and a dusk — every animation a known group's, keyframes only `transform`/`opacity`, every animated box within the viewport, no SMIL, the fire's frames — calm's set, the still census, a hidden page and a query frozen in place, the phase after a still, the theme to coffee leaving nothing behind, a cold night boot's first frame, screenshots in motion at five pinned moments, the larks in their clock's states).
