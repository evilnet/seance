What ships now: the same plains at the same hour, blurred and still behind the chat.

Stove in the middle

The yurt as it is built. The stove stands under the crown with a painted post on each side, and its pipe rises in front of the back window's middle bar. On a wide screen the newest lines sit right over the fire.

Stove at the far end

The same room with the eye turned left, so the stove stands where the yurt stands outside, 22% in from the far edge. Chat lines start at the left, so the fire is mostly clear of them.

Through the glass

A private message is always on glass now, so the room is only ever seen through it. "No glass" is for previewing the room: the chat sits straight on it, which the app never does. "As built" uses roughly today's tint and blur. "Lighter" thins the glass, and its legibility has not been measured. "Hide chat" shows the bare drawing.

Still by default

Like the frosted view, nothing moves. "Flicker" makes the fire breathe; it would be the only motion in a private message.

Drawn by hand in SVG from the theme's palette at noon, 18:00 and 21:00. The whole room is drawn in 3D from one eye just inside the door, so the round wall wraps around and the furniture turns with it. The phone crops the same room around the stove.
