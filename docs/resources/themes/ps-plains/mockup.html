The year: the date sets the season, and the season sets the plains. Spring is fresh green with flowers and flocks flying north; summer is deep and warm, shimmers at midday, and fills the far fields with fireflies after dusk; autumn turns the grass gold, brings the wind, and sends the flocks back south; winter lays snow on the ground and the yurt's roof. Each day draws its own weather from its season, and the sun keeps real hours — long days in June, short ones in December. The weather menu previews any of it on any date.

The words over the plains: dark ink with a pale halo in full daylight and light text at night, both of which you found easy.
